Research-Grade Analytical Framework

SHAND is engineered for academics, policy makers, and strategic designers who need to rigorously audit assumptions in critical arguments and plans. It provides a structured framework for examining the foundational elements of any narrative or proposal.

The tool emphasizes transparency, reproducibility, and methodological rigor in analysis. Every output is fully traceable to its source assumptions and logical rules, with no hidden variables or opaque processes.

Deterministic by Design: No machine learning, no external APIs, no statistical approximations. SHAND uses explicit logical rules, formal reasoning patterns, and transparent classification algorithms. Every conclusion is explainable, reproducible, and auditable.
