About SHAND

Understanding the philosophy, methodology, and purpose behind the Structured Human Assumption & Narrative Detector.

What is SHAND?

SHAND (Structured Human Assumption & Narrative Detector) is a research tool designed to make hidden assumptions in text visible, readable, and explorable. It is not a chatbot, not a landing page, and not a black-box AI. SHAND is built for slow, careful thinking.

Why Assumptions Matter

Assumptions are unstated beliefs or premises that shape decisions, plans, and arguments. Humans often miss them, leading to risk and misunderstanding. SHAND helps surface these assumptions for review and reflection before critical decisions are made.

Deterministic Design

SHAND uses deterministic, explainable logic. Every output is traceable and reviewable. No ML training, no external APIs, no hallucinations. Each analytical step follows explicit, transparent rules that can be examined and validated.

Research-Grade Analytical Framework

What is SHAND?

SHAND (Structured Human Assumption & Narrative Detector) is a research tool designed to make hidden assumptions in text visible, readable, and explorable. It is not a chatbot, not a landing page, and not a black-box AI. SHAND is built for slow, careful thinking.

Why assumptions matter

Assumptions are unstated beliefs or premises that shape decisions, plans, and arguments. Humans often miss them, leading to risk and misunderstanding. SHAND helps surface these assumptions for review and reflection.

Why SHAND avoids black-box AI

SHAND uses deterministic, explainable logic. Every output is traceable and reviewable. No ML training, no external APIs, no hallucinations. The tool follows explicit logical rules that can be examined at every step of the analysis process.

Research relevance

SHAND supports research in explainable risk assessment, narrative analysis, and cognitive audit. It is designed for academic, policy, and design review contexts where transparency and reproducibility are essential.

Core Philosophy: SHAND is built on the principle that analytical tools should enhance human reasoning rather than replace it. Every conclusion reached by SHAND can be traced back through explicit logical steps, making it a true partner in rigorous analysis rather than an opaque oracle.

Transparency

Every analytical step is visible and explainable. No hidden algorithms or opaque decision-making processes.

Reproducibility

Identical inputs produce identical outputs. No random variations or statistical approximations in the analysis.

Human-Centered

Designed to augment human intelligence, not replace it. The tool supports rather than supplants critical thinking.

Structured Output

Results are organized logically with clear relationships between assumptions, evidence, and conclusions.
